# Discrete Diffusion Models — Field Overview

Date: 2026-09-27
Scope: general discrete-state diffusion (theory + language + VQ/vision).

## The core idea

Diffusion models destroy structure by iteratively adding noise, then learn to reverse
it. Discrete diffusion generalizes this from continuous Gaussian noise to
**discrete / categorical state spaces** (tokens, VQ codes, labels, graphs). The forward
process is a Markov chain over a finite alphabet; the reverse process is learned to
denoise it.

## Two formalisms

| Family | Forward process | Training signal | Lineage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discrete-time Markov** | `q(x_t | x_{t-1}) = Cat(Q_t x_{t-1})`, fixed transition matrix `Q_t` | Variational ELBO (+ auxiliary cross-entropy) | Sohl-Dickstein 2015 → Hoogeboom 2021 → Austin 2021 (D3PM) |
| **Continuous-time (CTMC)** | CTMC with rate matrix `R_t`; `t` continuous | **Concrete score** = ratio `p(y)/p(x)` over neighbors → score-entropy loss | Meng 2022 (Concrete Score) → Lou 2024 (SEDD) |

These are not competing so much as the discrete-time discretization vs. the
continuous-time limit of the same underlying idea. D3PM is the practical framework;
concrete score / CTMC is the theory that makes the continuous-time objective tractable.

## The central design axis: the corruption kernel

- **Uniform** — every token → a uniformly random token (Multinomial Diffusion).
  Simple, closed-form, but weak; loses data structure fastest.
- **Absorbing / mask** — token → `[MASK]`. Bridges discrete diffusion to masked language
  modeling. Enables any-order generation, free infilling, and semi-autoregressive
  decoding (D3PM; MaskGIT; MDLM). Currently the dominant kernel in practice.
- **Structured** — e.g. Gaussian-like kernels on embeddings, or nearest-neighbor
  transitions in embedding space (D3PM). Encodes domain similarity but is more complex.

The choice of `Q_t` / `R_t` is the single most important design decision: it determines
what the model can reconstruct, how many steps sampling needs, and how the model relates
to AR/masked baselines.

## How it differs from continuous diffusion

- No Gaussian noise; corruption is a Markov transition on a finite alphabet.
- The score function is replaced by the **concrete score**, a ratio of probabilities of
  neighboring states under a neighborhood structure.
- The transition/rate matrix — not the noise schedule alone — is the core design object.
- Likelihood-based training (ELBO / score entropy) is viable and competitive; recent
  work closes much of the perplexity gap with autoregressive models.

## Short history / themes

- **2015** — Diffusion introduced, discrete (binomial) case included (Sohl-Dickstein).
- **2021** — Categorical diffusion formalized (Multinomial Diffusion); generalized to
  arbitrary transition matrices with the absorbing kernel (D3PM). Scaled to VQ latents
  (VQ-Diffusion) and masked image tokens (MaskGIT).
- **2022** — Missing theory supplied: concrete-score matching as the discrete analog of
  score matching (Meng et al.); continuous-time/continuous-input variants (CDCD).
- **2023–2024** — Results: likelihood scaling (Plaid 1B), score-entropy objective (SEDD),
  Rao-Blackwellized masked objective (MDLM) bring diffusion LMs to GPT-2-comparable
  perplexity with any-order / parallel decoding as the payoff.
- **Ongoing** — scaling and fine-tuning of diffusion LMs, efficient/adaptive samplers,
  guidance and controllable infilling, multimodal discrete diffusion.

## Applied track map (runner-ups)

Five papers carry the theory into practice and supply the important variants:

| Paper | Role | Kernel / method | Take-away |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VQ-Diffusion (2211.14822) | Vision | Mask-and-replace over VQ latents | Discrete diffusion scales to text-to-image; ~15× faster than AR |
| MaskGIT (2202.04200) | Vision | Iterative parallel masked-token refinement | Sampling schedule separable from diffusion formalism; up to 64× faster decoding |
| CDCD (2211.15089) | Language (alternative) | Continuous-time + continuous-input flow | Foil: continuous score model for categoricals without a CTMC |
| Plaid (2305.18619) | Language (likelihood) | Max-likelihood recipe + scaling laws | Diffusion LM can beat GPT-2 124M in likelihood; different scaling regime |
| MDLM (2406.07524) | Language (SOTA) | Rao-Blackwellized masked objective | Practical counterpart to SEDD; loss ≈ mixture of masked-LM losses |

Reading order suggestion: D3PM → SEDD + MDLM (the two current training recipes) →
Plaid (scaling) → VQ-Diffusion + MaskGIT (vision, sampling schedules) → CDCD (the
continuous alternative to contrast against).

## Open threads to track

1. Do diffusion LMs inherit the inference-efficiency wins at frontier scale, or does AR
   stay dominant? (KV-cache vs. fixed-step parallel decoding.)
2. Is the absorbing (mask) kernel close to optimal, or do structured kernels win under
   scale?
3. How do RLHF / preference fine-tuning recipes transfer to discrete diffusion models?
4. Relationship among ELBO, score entropy, and masked-LM objectives — are they the same
   estimator viewed differently?
